About Coffee Time

Crafting exceptional coffee experiences through ethical sourcing, artisan roasting, and pure passion in every cup.

OUR PASSION & HERITAGE

Every Cup Tells a Story of Craftsmanship

At Coffee Time, our journey began with a single vision: to bring the world's finest single-origin coffee beans directly from shade-grown highland farms to your morning cup.

From the sun-drenched terraced hills of Ethiopia and Colombia to the volcanic soils of Guatemala, we hand-select only top-grade 100% Arabica cherries. Our master roasters slow-roast every batch to unlock delicate aromas, notes of rich cocoa, citrus zest, and smooth caramel.

Our Core Values

Direct Trade Integrity
